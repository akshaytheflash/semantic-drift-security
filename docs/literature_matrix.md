## Gap statement

The intersection of (a) **real prohibition constraints with executable tool-level side
effects**, (b) **longitudinal accumulation within a single history**, (c) **action-level
and text-level measurement of the same events**, and (d) **reinforcement evaluated as a
dose** is not covered by any of the above. Each is covered individually by at least one
work; our contribution is their combination, on a preregistered protocol, and a comparison
of action-level with text-level verdicts under exactly the conditions prior work studies.
